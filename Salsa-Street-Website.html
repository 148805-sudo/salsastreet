About Us

Friendly, classic, and full of flavor.

Street food with a fun twist!

Salsa Street is a cheerful taco food-truck brand made for people who love tasty food and colorful design. Our style brings together a friendly taco mascot, bold orange lettering, and sunny colors.

From tacos and quesadillas to churros, chips, drinks, and street corn, Salsa Street is all about serving up a good time.
